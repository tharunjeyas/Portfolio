01 // Project Goal

Solving cardboard waste
at the source.

Primary Aim

To improve cardboard box waste management by implementing shredders in standard trash bins — reducing waste volume, maximising bin space, and preventing disposal of cardboard boxes outside the bins.

  • 01Analyse cardboard box waste management in Lithuania and through standard trash bins.
  • 02Develop a modification design in existing standard trash bins by implementing a shredder.
  • 03Investigate macro environmental factors for the implementation via PESTLE analysis.
  • 04Create a consumer survey to gather feedback on implementing changes in standard bins.
  • 05Evaluate market competition in the waste management segment and analyse product potential.
  • 06Investigate cost estimation to provide financial benefits for Kauno Švara.
